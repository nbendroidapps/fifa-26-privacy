Account and Data Deletion Guide

If you wish to delete your Football Quiz 2026 account and all associated data, you can do so directly from the mobile application or by contacting us.

Option 1: Delete via the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Football Quiz 2026 app on your device.
  2. Go to your Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap on Delete Account and Data.
  4. Confirm the deletion in the popup dialog.

Note: This action is permanent and will delete your high scores, unlocked trophies, and profile information from our servers immediately.

Option 2: Contact Support

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at bendroidappsn@gmail.com. Please include the email address associated with your Google account.
